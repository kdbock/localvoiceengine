# Crime story template

Use this for arrests, charges, traffic stops, investigations, court updates and public safety releases.

Target length: 25 to 40 seconds.

## Story promise

Tell what officials say happened, where it happened, who was charged if published and what readers need to know next.

## Script structure

1. What officials say happened.
2. Who was charged or what agency responded.
3. Location and timing.
4. Why the story matters or why readers asked about it.
5. Allegation reminder.
6. Closing line.

## Visual structure

1. RSS story image.
2. Charge card.
3. Location card.
4. Agency card.
5. Allegation card.
6. Branded end card.

## Required language

Charges are allegations unless proven in court.
