# Featured story template

Use this for profiles, community features, students, volunteers, small businesses, arts, sports and human-interest stories.

Target length: 35 to 55 seconds.

## Story promise

Tell the human story while still making the local news value clear.

## Script structure

1. Person and accomplishment.
2. Local connection.
3. Why the story matters now.
4. Quote, detail or scene.
5. Community connection.
6. Closing line.

## Visual structure

1. RSS story image or submitted photo.
2. Person card.
3. Quote card.
4. Place card.
5. Why it matters card.
6. Branded end card.
