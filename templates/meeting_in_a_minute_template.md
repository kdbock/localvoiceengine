# Meeting in a minute template

Use this for county commission, city council, school board and planning board meetings.

Target length: 45 to 60 seconds.

## Story promise

Tell residents what the board did, what it costs, who it affects and what happens next.

## Script structure

1. Biggest decision.
2. Reason, number or public impact.
3. Vote result or debate, if important.
4. Next hearing, deadline or public action.
5. Spending item.
6. Services item.
7. Grant, appointment or recognition.
8. Closing line.

## Visual structure

1. RSS story image or meeting location.
2. Vote card.
3. Number card.
4. Agenda card.
5. Money card.
6. Impact card.
7. Recognition or appointment card.
8. Branded end card.
