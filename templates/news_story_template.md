# News story template

Use this for general local updates, schools, roads, business, weather, deadlines, public notices and service changes.

Target length: 25 to 45 seconds.

## Story promise

Tell residents what changed, who is affected and what they should do next.

## Script structure

1. Main news.
2. Who is affected.
3. Date, place or amount.
4. What changes next.
5. Reader action, if any.
6. Closing line.

## Visual structure

1. RSS story image, if useful.
2. What changed card.
3. Date card.
4. Location card.
5. What to do card.
6. Branded end card.
