# Pexels background workflow

Pexels can supply ambient video backgrounds for Local Voice Engine packages.
Their license says photos and videos may be downloaded and used for free,
modified, and shared on social media. Attribution is not required, though it is
appreciated.

Sources checked Sept. 9, 2026:

- https://www.pexels.com/license/
- https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/ambient%20background/

## How to use Pexels safely

Download useful clips into:

`assets/backgrounds/`

Then add the filename or path to a story package:

```json
"ambient_asset": "assets/backgrounds/courthouse-ambient.mp4"
```

## Recommended searches

- courthouse exterior
- city hall
- local government meeting
- police lights abstract
- documents desk
- newspaper background
- map background
- small business storefront
- highway road
- school hallway empty
- library books

## Sensitive-story rules

- Avoid identifiable people in crime, death, court, politics or child welfare
  stories unless the image is directly relevant and editor-approved.
- Do not imply a person, business or brand in stock footage is connected to the
  story.
- Use abstract or location-neutral motion for arrests and investigations.
- Prefer article photos, public meeting footage or local location images when
  available.
