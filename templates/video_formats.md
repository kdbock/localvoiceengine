# Neuse News video formats

These are the repeatable formats for Local Voice Engine. Use the four core
templates first, then treat the extra formats as occasional specialty formats.

## Meeting in a minute

Use after city council, county commission, school board and planning board
meetings.

Opening pattern:
"[Board name] approved [main action]. Here are the other decisions residents should know."

Best for:
Taxes, zoning, roads, schools, public safety, utilities, grants, appointments
and spending.

Target length:
45 to 60 seconds.

## Crime story

Use for arrests, charges, traffic stops, investigations, court updates and
public safety releases.

Opening pattern:
"[Agency] says [what happened] at [location]."

Best for:
Crime, public safety, traffic stops, arrests, charges and follow-up questions
from readers.

Required language:
"Charges are allegations unless proven in court."

Target length:
25 to 40 seconds.

## News story

Use for practical local updates.

Opening pattern:
"[What changed] in [place]. Here is what residents need to know."

Best for:
Weather, schools, roads, public hearings, closures, health updates, service
changes, deadlines and local business updates.

Target length:
25 to 45 seconds.

## Featured story

Use for people-centered features.

Opening pattern:
"[Person or group] is [plain-language accomplishment or story]."

Best for:
Students, teachers, volunteers, athletes, small-business owners, artists,
community leaders and human-interest stories.

Target length:
35 to 55 seconds.

## Specialty formats

### What happened next

Use for stories that generated questions, comments or follow-up interest.

Opening pattern:
"If you saw questions about [event], here is what officials say happened."

Best for:
Road closures, government follow-ups, business openings, investigations and
reader-submitted questions.

### Three things to know

Use for practical local updates.

Opening pattern:
"Here are three things to know in [county/city] today."

Best for:
Weather, DMV changes, public hearings, school schedules, trash pickup, closures,
health updates and deadlines.

### New and coming soon

Use for local business openings, closings, expansions and construction.

Opening pattern:
"A [city] business is expanding. Here is what we know."

Best for:
Restaurants, stores, permits, hiring notices and development projects.

### Public records roundup

Use for recurring records that readers already check.

Opening pattern:
"Here are this week's public records from [county]."

Best for:
New corporations, land transfers, inspections, permits and filings.

### Faces of the story

Use for people-centered features.

Opening pattern:
"At [age/context], [person] is [plain-language accomplishment]."

Best for:
Students, teachers, volunteers, athletes, small-business owners and community
leaders.

### Election 2026 explainer

Use for civic education and election coverage.

Opening pattern:
"Before you vote in [race], here is what this office actually does."

Best for:
Candidate profiles, offices, voting deadlines, district maps, forums and issue
explainers.
