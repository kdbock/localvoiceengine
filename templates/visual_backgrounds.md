# Visual background guide

## Crime and public safety

Use:
- map pin or street view still
- agency-provided photo
- neutral police lights background
- timeline cards
- document screenshot with sensitive details removed

Avoid:
- dramatic music
- mugshot-heavy edits unless editorially necessary
- speculation or guilt-implying language

## Government meetings

Use:
- city hall or courthouse photo
- agenda screenshot
- boardroom still
- county map
- simple vote or dollar amount card

## Public records

Use:
- clean table animation
- county map
- highlighted record cards
- office exterior or seal when appropriate

## Business openings

Use:
- storefront photo
- food or product photo
- map route
- opening date card

## Human features

Use:
- submitted photos
- slow zooms
- location B-roll
- quote cards

## Election and civic explainers

Use:
- ballot or polling place imagery
- district map
- office title card
- candidate headshots when coverage is balanced and approved
